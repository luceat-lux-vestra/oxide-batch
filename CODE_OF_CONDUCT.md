# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OxideBatch a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`, with
`Code of Conduct` in the subject or report title. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation.
